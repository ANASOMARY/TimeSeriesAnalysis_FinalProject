How this is worked out

  • Read means every paragraph on the page has been covered: on the reviewer's screen, in focus, for at least as long as a very fast reader would need; ticked by the reviewer; or commented on. Text that was only loaded or scrolled past does not count. Each reviewer's uncovered paragraphs are listed from the chapter's page.
  • Pace is pages a day over the last {{ pace_window }} days, or since the chapters were assigned if that is more recent. Days without reading count, so going quiet lowers it.
  • Projected finish is today plus the pages left divided by that pace.{% if p.target %} Needed is the pages left divided by the days to the target.{% endif %}
    • {% if p.target %}
  • On track: the pace meets what is needed. At risk: at least {{ at_risk_share }}% of it. Behind: less than that, including no recent reading.
